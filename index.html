4 — Reconstruct and translate a Tonnetz configuration

Ear-to-space exercise
Hear a triadic configuration, reproduce it on the lattice, then translate the successful shape. The second step is essential: the shape moves while its internal interval content remains invariant.

5 — Translate and reconstruct patterns on a Harpejji-style surface

Main idea
The surface is isomorphic: moving a rigid shape left/right or up/down transposes every pitch by the same amount. Horizontal motion is +2 semitones and vertical motion is +1 semitone. Absolute register is preserved; there is no octave wrapping.

There is also positional redundancy. Because one horizontal step (+2) can be cancelled by two vertical steps in the opposite direction (-2), the very same absolute pitches can often be played in several different places. The instrument therefore combines translation invariance with multiple equivalent fingerings.
Challenge mode: hear a hidden triad shape, then reproduce its interval pattern anywhere on the surface.
